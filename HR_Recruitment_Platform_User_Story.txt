OVERVIEW & VISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This document is the definitive user story reference for the HR Recruitment
Management System (ATS — Applicant Tracking System), a web-based recruitment
platform designed to streamline the end-to-end hiring workflow. The system
serves multiple stakeholder groups: job-seeking candidates building their
careers, company administrators managing organizational hiring needs, internal
company users (recruiters and interviewers) operating within the hiring pipeline,
and a super admin overseeing the entire platform ecosystem.

The platform is built with a clean, minimalist philosophy — every screen is
intentional, every interaction is purposeful, and every data point is meaningful
to the user consuming it. The system is designed to eliminate manual tracking,
reduce hiring delays, and bring clarity to what is often a chaotic, spreadsheet-
driven hiring process. It is a lean yet scalable solution suitable for small to
mid-sized organizations looking for a structured and modern approach to talent
acquisition.

The entire application is organized across 8 core functional modules:
  1. Authentication & Authorization
  2. Company & User Management
  3. Job Management
  4. Candidate Management
  5. Application & Hiring Pipeline
  6. Interview Management
  7. Notifications System
  8. Analytics Dashboard

Each module described in this document is narrated from the perspective of the
user experiencing it — how they arrive, what they see, what they do, and what
they feel. This narrative is supplemented by specific UI requirements, data
fields, interaction rules, and enhancement suggestions to guide any UI agent or
designer building the application pages.

MODULE 1 — AUTHENTICATION & AUTHORIZATION
Landing Page · Login · Signup · OAuth · Password Recovery
================================================================================

THE STORY BEGINS HERE.

A first-time visitor arrives at the platform's root URL and is greeted by a
minimalistic, clean landing page. The page does not overwhelm. There is no
lengthy copy, no animated carousels, no feature grids. The primary real estate
of the landing page is dominated by a single, powerful CTA: a call to action
that asks the visitor whether they are here as an Internal User (a company or
its staff) or as a Candidate looking for a job. The left half of the screen
surfaces the pain points of traditional HR processes — fragmented spreadsheets,
lost candidate data, missed interviews, zero pipeline visibility — framed as
relatable problems that this platform solves. The right half holds the login
and signup modal, which dynamically switches between two personas.

--- LANDING PAGE UI REQUIREMENTS ---
  - Split-screen layout (50/50 or 60/40 favoring the right)
  - Left side: Pain point messaging — short, punchy, 3-4 sentences max
  - Right side: Login modal with a toggle to switch between "Internal" and
    "Candidate" personas
  - The toggle is prominent — a pill/tab switcher at the top of the modal
  - No navbar clutter; the page is intentionally sparse
  - Background: subtle gradient or soft texture — avoid pure white
  - Logo at top-left; minimal footer at the bottom

--- INTERNAL LOGIN (Company Admin / Recruiter / Interviewer) ---
  - Email input field
  - Password input field (show/hide toggle)
  - "Forgot Password?" link below password field
  - "Login with Google" button (Google OAuth)
  - "Don't have an account? Sign up" link that transitions to signup mode
  - Validation: email format check, empty field checks

--- CANDIDATE LOGIN ---
  - Email input field
  - Password input field (show/hide toggle)
  - "Forgot Password?" link
  - "Login with Google" button (Google OAuth)
  - "New here? Sign up" link that transitions to candidate signup mode

--- INTERNAL SIGNUP (Company Admin) ---
  Fields collected:
    Company Details:
      · Company Name (required)
      · Company Domain (required, e.g., acme.com)
      · Company Size (optional — dropdown: 1-10, 11-50, 51-200, 201-500, 500+)
      · Industry (optional — dropdown: Technology, Finance, Healthcare, etc.)
      · Address (required)
      · Contact Email (required)
    Admin User Details:
      · Full Name (required)
      · Email (required)
      · Password (required, strength indicator)
      · Confirm Password (required, match validation)
  - After submission: Company marked as "Pending" awaiting Super Admin approval
  - A confirmation email is sent to the admin email

--- CANDIDATE SIGNUP ---
  Fields collected:
    · Full Name (required)
    · Email (required, unique)
    · Phone Number (required)
    · Location (optional)
    · Skills (optional — tag-input with suggestions)
    · Password (required, strength indicator)
    · Confirm Password (required)
  - After submission: Candidate redirected to 4-step Onboarding Wizard
  - Google OAuth signup also redirects to the same wizard

--- FORGOT PASSWORD FLOW ---
  Step 1: User enters their registered email address
  Step 2: System sends a password reset link to that email
  Step 3: User clicks the link and lands on a reset password page
  Step 4: User enters new password + confirm password
  Step 5: On success, user is redirected to login with a success toast

--- ENHANCEMENT SUGGESTIONS ---
  · Consider adding animated transitions between Internal and Candidate modes
    on the landing page toggle — a smooth slide or fade makes the experience
    feel premium.
  · Add a "Remember Me" checkbox on the login form to improve returning user
    experience.
  · On the landing page left side, consider a subtle animated loop — perhaps
    a slow-moving Kanban board preview or a hiring pipeline visualization — to
    visually communicate the product's core value without words.
  · Password strength meter should be color-coded: red → orange → green
  · Show tooltip guidance for Domain field (e.g., "Enter your company's domain
    without 'www', e.g., techcorp.com")

MODULE 2 — COMPANY ONBOARDING WIZARD
Route: /company/onboarding | /company/onboarding/step/1 | /step/2
================================================================================

After the Company Admin completes signup and their account is approved by the
Super Admin, they are redirected to the Company Onboarding Wizard. This is a
focused, distraction-free two-section wizard form that walks the administrator
through setting up the full company profile. The wizard is built to be resumable
— if the admin closes the tab, refreshes, or decides to come back later, all
progress is saved at the current step. The URL changes per step (/step/1,
/step/2) so the browser history supports navigation.

The wizard opens on a clean, centered card layout. A progress indicator at the
top shows the two steps with step labels. There is no sidebar here — the full
focus is on completing the company setup.

--- WIZARD STEP 1: COMPANY PROFILE ---
  Fields:
    · Company Name (pre-filled from signup, editable)
    · Company Domain (pre-filled, editable)
    · Company Size (dropdown: 1-10, 11-50, 51-200, 201-500, 500+)
    · Industry (dropdown with popular categories)
    · Company Logo Upload (optional — drag and drop or file picker, accepts PNG/JPG)
    · Company Bio / Description (optional — textarea, max 500 characters)
    · Website URL (optional)
  Navigation: "Save & Continue" button → goes to Step 2
              "Save Progress" link → saves and stays on current step

--- WIZARD STEP 2: LOCATION & CONTACT ---
  Fields:
    · Address Line 1 (required)
    · Address Line 2 (optional)
    · City (required)
    · State / Province (required)
    · Country (required — dropdown)
    · Postal Code (required)
    · Contact Email (pre-filled from signup, editable)
    · Contact Phone Number (optional)
  Navigation: "Back" → Step 1
              "Complete Setup" → Submits wizard, redirects to Company Dashboard

--- ENHANCEMENT SUGGESTIONS ---
  · Add a third optional step for "Branding & Appearance" where admins can
    choose an accent color for their company's internal view — small but creates
    a sense of ownership.
  · Show a real-time preview panel on the right side of the wizard that renders
    a company profile card as the user fills in details — this "see it live"
    interaction significantly improves completion rates.
  · Show a progress percentage badge (e.g., "40% complete") alongside the step
    indicators to encourage completion.
  · Add inline field-level hints (e.g., "This will be visible to candidates
    on job postings") to reduce confusion about why certain data is needed.

MODULE 3 — CANDIDATE ONBOARDING WIZARD
Route: /candidate/onboarding | /step/1 through /step/4
================================================================================

When a candidate successfully registers — whether through email signup or Google
OAuth — they are redirected immediately to the Candidate Onboarding Wizard. This
is a four-step wizard that builds out the candidate's full profile. This wizard
is resumable: the candidate can skip ahead, close the page, and return at any
point to continue from where they left off. Each step saves progress independently
via its own API endpoint.

The wizard is designed to feel more like a personal introduction than a form-
filling exercise. The copy at each step is warm and encouraging. The layout is
clean, the fields are generously spaced, and the progress bar at the top clearly
communicates where the candidate stands.

--- WIZARD STEP 1: PERSONAL DETAILS ---
  Fields:
    · Avatar Selection: 6 pre-built illustrated avatars displayed as a grid of
      circular swatches. Candidate clicks to select one. No custom upload.
    · Full Name (pre-filled from signup, editable)
    · Phone Number (required if not entered at signup)
    · Location / City (text input)
    · Date of Birth (optional)
    · A short "About Me" text (optional, 2-3 sentences encouraged)
  UX Notes:
    - Avatar selection should have a clear "selected" ring animation
    - Step title: "Let's get to know you"

--- WIZARD STEP 2: RESUME & SUMMARY ---
  Fields:
    · Resume Upload: File picker accepting PDF only, max 5MB
      - Drag-and-drop zone with clear instructions
      - File name displayed after upload with a "Remove" option
    · Resume Drive Link: Alternate option — paste a Google Drive / Dropbox link
      - Toggle between "Upload File" and "Paste Link" using a tab switcher
    · Professional Summary: Rich-text or simple textarea editor
      - Placeholder copy: "Briefly describe your professional background,
        what you're good at, and what you're looking for"
      - Max 1000 characters
  UX Notes:
    - Show upload progress bar during file upload
    - Validate file type before upload — show friendly error if non-PDF
    - Step title: "Your resume & story"

--- WIZARD STEP 3: PROFESSIONAL LINKS ---
  Fields:
    · GitHub Profile URL (text input with GitHub icon)
    · LeetCode Profile URL (text input with LeetCode icon)
    · LinkedIn Profile URL (text input with LinkedIn icon)
    · Portfolio / Personal Website URL (text input)
    · Any Other Link (labeled "Other" — free text)
  UX Notes:
    - Each field shows its platform icon on the left
    - URL validation on blur (not on every keystroke)
    - Step title: "Where can we find your work?"
    - All fields are optional but the step itself is not skippable without
      acknowledging it

--- WIZARD STEP 4: JOB PREFERENCES ---
  Fields:
    · Skills: Tag-input field
      - Candidate types skills and hits Enter/comma to add tags
      - Suggestions appear from a pre-defined skills bank
      - Visual skill tags displayed below the input
    · Minimum Expected Salary: Number input (in local currency or USD)
    · Maximum Expected Salary: Number input
    · Salary Display Note: "Salary range is used for job matching only and
      is not shown publicly"
    · Job Type Preference: Multi-select chips
      Options: Full-Time | Part-Time | Contract | Internship | Remote
    · Preferred Role / Job Title (free text or dropdown)
    · Preferred Location (text input or "Open to Relocation" toggle)
  UX Notes:
    - Salary inputs should be numeric-only with comma formatting
    - Job type chips should be togglable (selected = filled, unselected = outline)
    - Step title: "What are you looking for?"
    - "Complete Profile" button submits and redirects to Candidate Dashboard

--- ENHANCEMENT SUGGESTIONS ---
  · Add a profile completion percentage ring/circle that updates dynamically
    as the candidate fills in each field — visible throughout the wizard.
  · After wizard completion, show a "Your profile is ready!" celebration screen
    (a micro-animation with confetti or a checkmark pulse) before redirecting
    to the dashboard. This small moment of delight significantly improves
    first-session engagement.
  · In Step 4, consider adding a "Skills Match Preview" — a subtle badge that
    shows how many jobs on the platform currently match the entered skills.
    This creates immediate motivation and curiosity.
  · Allow candidates to go back to any step freely using the progress bar as
    a navigation element (click Step 2 to jump directly back to Step 2).

MODULE 4 — CANDIDATE DASHBOARD & CANDIDATE PAGES
Routes: /candidate/dashboard | /candidate/jobs | /candidate/applications
        /candidate/interviews | /candidate/profile
================================================================================

Once a candidate completes their onboarding (or skips it and returns later),
they land on the Candidate Dashboard. The candidate experience is anchored by a
top navigation bar — clean, horizontal, minimal — with links to the four primary
sections: Profile, Jobs, Applications, and Interviews. There is no sidebar here.
The layout is airy, with generous white space, and the visual hierarchy guides
the candidate's attention to what matters most in each view.

The candidate's journey through this platform is a continuous loop: build profile
→ find jobs → apply → track applications → attend interviews → receive outcome.
Every page in the candidate view is designed to support one step in this loop.

--- CANDIDATE DASHBOARD ---
  The main dashboard page serves as an overview hub.
  Key UI Elements:
    · Profile Completion Bar: A horizontal progress bar with percentage.
      If below 80%, a prompt card urges the candidate to complete their profile.
    · Recent Applications: A horizontal scroll or stacked card list showing the
      3 most recent job applications with their current pipeline status
      (Applied, Screening, Interview, Offer, etc.)
    · Upcoming Interviews: A card panel showing the next 1-2 scheduled
      interviews with date, time, round type, and a "Join Interview" button
      (visible when interview is within 30 minutes of scheduled time)
    · Recommended Jobs: 3-4 job cards surfaced based on the candidate's
      listed skills and preferences
    · Quick Stats Row:
        - Total Applications Submitted
        - Active Applications (not rejected/withdrawn)
        - Interviews Scheduled
        - Profile Strength Score

--- CANDIDATE JOBS PAGE (/candidate/jobs) ---
  A full job listing page where candidates browse and discover open positions.
  Layout: Grid of job cards (2-3 per row on desktop, 1 per row on mobile)

  Each Job Card Displays:
    · Job Title
    · Company Name + Company Logo/Avatar
    · Location (or "Remote")
    · Employment Type (Full-Time / Part-Time / Contract)
    · Required Skills (shown as tags, max 4 visible, "+N more" overflow)
    · Date Posted (relative: "3 days ago")
    · A subtle "Apply Now" button or "Already Applied" badge

  Search & Filter Panel (left sidebar or top filter bar):
    · Keyword search (matches title, description, skills)
    · Location filter (text input or dropdown)
    · Job Type filter (Full-Time / Part-Time / Contract / Remote — multi-select)
    · Experience Level filter (Entry / Mid / Senior)
    · Skills filter (tag-based multi-select)
    · Date Posted filter (Today / Past Week / Past Month)
    · Salary Range slider (optional)

  Pagination: Load more button or page-based pagination at bottom

  Job Detail View (opens as a side drawer or full page):
    · Full Job Title and Company Name
    · Job Description (full text, formatted)
    · Required Skills (tags)
    · Department, Location, Experience Level
    · Salary Range (if provided)
    · Application Deadline
    · "Apply Now" button → opens an Apply modal
    · Apply Modal contains:
        - Resume to use: shows uploaded resume or allows re-upload
        - Cover Note (optional textarea, max 500 chars)
        - Confirm & Submit button
    · If already applied: shows application status badge instead of Apply button

--- CANDIDATE APPLICATIONS PAGE (/candidate/applications) ---
  A list view of all jobs the candidate has applied to.
  Layout: Table-style list (not cards) with key data columns

  Columns:
    · Job Title (clickable → opens job detail)
    · Company Name
    · Date Applied
    · Current Pipeline Stage (color-coded badge):
        Applied → Screening → Shortlisted → Interview 1 → Interview 2 →
        Offer → Hired → Rejected
    · Last Updated (relative date)
    · Actions: View Details | Withdraw Application

  Filters:
    · Filter by Status (dropdown)
    · Search by job title
    · Date range filter

  Application Detail Drawer/Page:
    · Full application details including resume snapshot
    · Pipeline stage history (timeline view showing stage transitions with dates)
    · Interview schedule linked to this application (if any)
    · Withdraw button (only if status is not Hired or Rejected)

--- CANDIDATE INTERVIEWS PAGE (/candidate/interviews) ---
  A timeline-style or list view of all scheduled interviews.
  Layout: Vertical list of interview cards, sorted by scheduled date

  Each Interview Card Displays:
    · Job Title it belongs to
    · Company Name
    · Round Type (Phone Screen / Technical / Behavioral / HR / Final)
    · Scheduled Date and Time
    · Duration (e.g., "45 minutes")
    · Interviewer Name (if disclosed)
    · Status (Scheduled / Completed / Cancelled)
    · Join Interview button (active 30 minutes before scheduled time)
    · Meeting Link (if not using the platform's built-in workspace)

  Filters:
    · Filter by status (Upcoming / Completed / Cancelled)
    · Filter by round type

--- CANDIDATE PROFILE PAGE (/candidate/profile) ---
  A comprehensive, structured page that displays and allows editing of the full
  candidate profile. This page is derived from the data collected in the
  onboarding wizard but extends it further.

  Page Sections:
    · Header Section:
        - Avatar (one of the 6 pre-selected)
        - Full Name
        - Current Job Preference / Target Role
        - Profile Completion Percentage (circular progress ring)
        - Edit Profile button (top right)

    · Contact Information:
        - Email | Phone | Location
        - Inline edit or modal edit

    · Professional Links:
        - GitHub | LeetCode | LinkedIn | Portfolio | Other
        - Each shown as a clickable chip with platform icon

    · Resume Section:
        - Current resume file name with download icon
        - Upload Date
        - Re-upload button | Delete button
        - Drive Link (if provided)
        - Resume Preview button (opens PDF in modal)

    · Professional Summary:
        - Displayed as a formatted paragraph
        - Edit icon to open inline editor

    · Skills:
        - Skill tags listed in a horizontal wrap
        - Add/Remove skill interface

    · Work Experience (optional — can be populated from parsed resume data):
        - Each entry: Company, Role, Duration, Description
        - "Add Experience" button for manual entry

    · Preferences:
        - Salary Range displayed
        - Job Type preferences (chips)
        - Preferred Location

--- ENHANCEMENT SUGGESTIONS ---
  · On the Candidate Dashboard, add a "Profile Strength Score" section with
    a breakdown: 5 sub-categories (Profile Info, Resume, Links, Skills,
    Preferences) each shown as a mini progress bar. This gamifies profile
    completion and drives higher-quality candidate data.
  · On the Jobs page, add an AI Match Score badge on each job card (e.g.,
    "87% Match") calculated from the candidate's skills vs. job required skills.
    This is already in the data model (ai_match_score field) and makes the
    experience feel intelligent and personalized.
  · Add a "Bookmarked Jobs" section — candidates can heart/bookmark jobs they
    are interested in but not ready to apply to yet. This is a low-effort
    feature with high engagement impact.
  · For the Applications page, show an animated pipeline stage indicator (like
    a stepper component) inside each application detail so candidates can
    visually understand where they stand in the hiring process.
  · Add push notification badges on the top nav icons (Applications, Interviews)
    when there are status updates — even basic badge counts improve return visits.

MODULE 5 — COMPANY DASHBOARD & COMPANY MODULE
Routes: /company/dashboard | /company/users | /company/jobs | /company/candidates
        /company/applications | /company/interviews | /company/feedback
================================================================================

After a Company Admin or any internal user (Recruiter or Interviewer) logs in
through the Internal Login, they arrive at the Company Dashboard. The company
view is structured around a persistent left sidebar that provides navigation to
all key sections. The sidebar is clean, icon-plus-label style, with the company
logo or name at the top and a user avatar + role badge at the bottom.

The company module is the operational heart of the platform — it is where the
entire hiring machine runs. Recruiters post jobs, review candidates, manage
applications, schedule interviews, and track outcomes. Interviewers view their
assigned sessions and submit feedback. Company Admins oversee all of this and
manage the team.

--- SIDEBAR NAVIGATION ---
  Items (in order):
    · Dashboard (home icon)
    · Users (people icon)
    · Jobs (briefcase icon)
    · Candidates (person-search icon)
    · Applications (document-stack icon)
    · Interviews (calendar icon)
    · Feedback (star/comment icon)
  Bottom:
    · Settings (gear icon — company settings)
    · Logout (exit icon)
  Active state: highlighted background on the current section's nav item

--- COMPANY DASHBOARD (/company/dashboard) ---
  A minimalist statistics hub that gives a quick pulse of the hiring activity.
  It should NOT be cluttered. Every element earns its place.

  Top Row — Key Metric Cards (4 cards):
    · Total Jobs Posted
    · Active Jobs (status = published)
    · Total Candidates (who applied to this company's jobs)
    · Total Applications

  Mid Row — Interview Summary Row (3 cards):
    · Total Interviews Scheduled
    · Completed Interviews
    · Interview Conversion Rate (Interviewed → Hired %)

  Bottom Row — Hiring Summary (2 cards):
    · Total Hires
    · Rejection Rate

  Charts Section (2-3 charts, not more):
    · Bar Chart: Applications per Job (top 5-6 jobs by applicant count)
    · Donut/Pie Chart: Pipeline Stage Distribution
      (how many candidates are at Applied / Screening / Interview / Offer / Hired)
    · Line Chart: Applications over time (past 30 days, weekly)

  Recent Activity Feed (optional right column):
    · Last 5 actions in the system (e.g., "Jane applied to Frontend Engineer",
      "Interview scheduled for Mark — Technical Round")

--- COMPANY USERS PAGE (/company/users) ---
  A list of all internal users (Recruiters and Interviewers) belonging to
  this company. This page is accessible only to Company Admins.

  Layout: Table/list format (Excel-style rows)

  Columns:
    · Name
    · Email
    · Role (Recruiter / Interviewer)
    · Date Invited
    · Status (Active / Pending / Deactivated)
    · Actions (Edit | Deactivate | Remove)

  Search: Search by name or email
  Filter: Filter by Role (Recruiter / Interviewer) | Filter by Status

  "Invite User" Button (top right):
    Opens a modal with:
      · Full Name (required)
      · Email (required)
      · Role Dropdown: Recruiter | Interviewer (required)
      · Password (required — temporary password set by admin)
      · Confirm Password (required)
    On submit:
      - System sends an invite email to the provided email address
      - Email contains login credentials and a link to the internal login page
      - User appears in the list with "Pending" status until they first log in

--- COMPANY JOBS PAGE (/company/jobs) ---
  A grid of job cards representing all jobs posted by the company.
  Layout: Square card grid (3 per row on desktop, 2 on tablet, 1 on mobile)

  Each Job Card Displays:
    · Job Title (bold, prominent)
    · Department
    · Location
    · Employment Type
    · Status Badge: Draft (grey) | Published (green) | Closed (red)
    · Number of Applicants
    · Date Created
    · Action icons: Edit (pencil) | View (eye) | Delete (trash)

  Search: Keyword search bar
  Filter: Status filter (Draft / Published / Closed) | Department filter

  "Create Job" Button (top right):
    Opens a full form (modal or new page) with:
      · Job Title (required)
      · Job Description (rich text editor — required)
      · Department (dropdown or free text)
      · Location (text input + "Remote" toggle)
      · Employment Type (dropdown: Full-Time / Part-Time / Contract / Internship)
      · Experience Level (dropdown: Entry / Mid / Senior)
      · Required Skills (tag input)
      · Salary Range Min (number)
      · Salary Range Max (number)
      · Application Deadline (date picker)
      · Job Status (Draft / Published)
    On save:
      - Job appears in the grid
      - If Published, it immediately appears on the candidate job board

--- COMPANY CANDIDATES PAGE (/company/candidates) ---
  A list of all candidates who have applied to at least one job at this company.
  Layout: Table/list (Excel-style with rows and columns)

  Columns:
    · Candidate Name
    · Email
    · Phone
    · Applied Jobs (count or list of job titles)
    · Skills (top 3 skills, "+N more" overflow)
    · Overall Status (most advanced pipeline stage across all applications)
    · AI Match Score (if computed)
    · Date First Applied
    · Actions: View Profile

  Search: Name, email, skill keyword
  Filter: By status | By job | By skill

  Note: There is NO "Add Candidate" button here. Candidates are only added
  through self-application or through the Applications module.

  Candidate Detail View (opens as drawer or new page):
    · Full profile card (avatar, name, contact, links)
    · All applications from this candidate to this company
    · Resume download link
    · Skills list
    · AI Match Score

--- COMPANY APPLICATIONS PAGE (/company/applications) ---
  The central pipeline management page. This is where the hiring action happens.
  Layout: Table list view (primary) with optional Kanban board toggle

  Table Columns:
    · Candidate Name
    · Job Title
    · Current Pipeline Stage (color-coded badge)
    · Assigned Recruiter
    · Assigned Interviewer
    · AI Score
    · Date Applied
    · Last Updated
    · Actions: View | Move Stage | Schedule Interview | Reject

  Pipeline Stages (in order):
    Applied → Screening → Shortlisted → Interview 1 → Interview 2 →
    Offer → Hired / Rejected

  Search: Candidate name or job title
  Filter: By stage | By job | By recruiter | By date range

  Kanban Board Toggle (optional enhanced view):
    - Columns represent each pipeline stage
    - Candidate cards can be dragged between stages
    - Each card shows: Candidate name, Job title, Applied date, AI score

  "Add Application" Button (top right):
    Opens a modal with:
      · Select Candidate (searchable dropdown of existing candidates or new entry)
      · Select Job (searchable dropdown of published jobs)
      · Cover Note (optional)
      · Initial Pipeline Stage (defaults to "Applied")
      · Assign Recruiter (dropdown of company's recruiters)
      · Assign Interviewer (dropdown of company's interviewers)

  Application Detail View:
    · Full candidate profile snapshot
    · Pipeline stage history (timestamped transitions)
    · Interview history linked to this application
    · Feedback history from interviewers
    · Move to next stage / Reject buttons
    · Notes section (recruiter internal notes — not visible to candidate)

--- COMPANY INTERVIEWS PAGE (/company/interviews) ---
  A list of all interviews scheduled across all jobs and candidates.
  Layout: Table/list with rows

  Columns:
    · Candidate Name
    · Job Title
    · Round Type (Phone / Screening / Technical / Behavioral / HR / Final)
    · Assigned Interviewer
    · Scheduled Date & Time
    · Duration
    · Status (Scheduled / Completed / Cancelled)
    · Actions: View | Reschedule | Cancel | Join

  Search: By candidate name or job title
  Filter: By status | By round type | By interviewer | By date range

  "Schedule Interview" Button (top right):
    Opens a modal with:
      · Select Application (searchable — links to a specific candidate+job pair)
      · Round Type (dropdown)
      · Select Interviewer (dropdown of company's interviewers)
      · Date (date picker)
      · Time (time picker)
      · Duration (dropdown: 30 min / 45 min / 60 min / 90 min)
      · Meeting Link (optional — Zoom/Meet URL; if blank, platform workspace used)
      · Status (defaults to "Scheduled")
    On save:
      - Interview is created
      - Notification email sent to candidate and interviewer

  Interview Workspace Link:
    - Clicking "Join" on an interview row opens the Live Interview Workspace
      (see Module 6 extension — Live Interview Workspace)

--- COMPANY FEEDBACK PAGE (/company/feedback) ---
  A consolidated view of all feedback submitted by interviewers.
  Layout: Table/list

  Columns:
    · Candidate Name
    · Job Title
    · Round Type
    · Interviewer Name
    · Rating (1-5 star display)
    · Recommendation (Strong Hire / Hire / No Hire / Strong No Hire)
    · Date Submitted
    · Actions: View Full Feedback

  Feedback Detail View (drawer or modal):
    · Candidate + Interview details at top
    · Rating (star display)
    · Strengths (text)
    · Weaknesses (text)
    · Recommendation badge
    · Submitted by (interviewer name + date)

MODULE 7 — LIVE INTERVIEW WORKSPACE (EXTENSION)
Route: /interview/:id/workspace
================================================================================

The Live Interview Workspace is the most technically sophisticated page in the
platform. It is accessed via a "Join Interview" button that becomes active for
both candidates and interviewers 30 minutes before the scheduled interview time.
The workspace experience differs based on role and round type.

--- CANDIDATE VIEW — TECHNICAL ROUND ---
  Layout: Split-screen (two equal panels side by side)

  Left Panel — Video Call:
    · Jitsi Meet embedded in an iframe
    · Room name: interview_{interview_id} (unique per interview)
    · Auto-joins on page load — no lobby screen
    · Audio and video enabled by default
    · Controls visible: Microphone toggle | Camera toggle | Chat | Hang Up
    · Jitsi branding and unnecessary toolbar items hidden
    · The panel is resizable (drag the divider)

  Right Panel — Code Editor:
    · Monaco Editor (same engine as VS Code) embedded
    · Language selector dropdown at top (50+ languages)
    · Line numbers and minimap enabled
    · Light/Dark theme toggle
    · Auto-save every 5 seconds (code persisted server-side per session)
    · Run Code button at bottom right
    · Output Console below the editor:
        - Shows stdout, stderr
        - Supports STDIN input (text field for providing input)
        - Execution time and memory usage shown
    · Code execution powered by JDoodle API via backend proxy
    · Rate limit: 10 executions per minute per session
    · Max execution time: 15 seconds | Memory: 256 MB

--- INTERVIEWER VIEW — TECHNICAL ROUND ---
  Layout: Full-screen Jitsi Meet video call
  · Same room as the candidate (interview_{interview_id})
  · The code editor is NOT visible to the interviewer
  · Interviewer focuses purely on verbal conversation and problem discussion
  · Preparation notes (pre-filled by recruiter) shown in a small collapsible
    panel below the video — reference only, doesn't distract from the call

--- CANDIDATE VIEW — APTITUDE / MCQ ROUND ---
  Layout: Full-screen assessment panel

  Components:
    · Question counter: "Question 4 of 20"
    · Countdown timer (prominent, top right — color changes to red below 10%)
    · Question text displayed clearly
    · Answer options (MCQ radio buttons or checkbox for multi-select)
    · Previous / Next navigation buttons
    · Question navigator (mini grid showing answered/unanswered/flagged)
    · Flag for Review button (marks question to revisit)
    · Auto-submit when timer reaches zero
    · Manual Submit button (with confirmation modal to prevent accidental submit)

  Post-submission:
    · "Assessment Submitted" confirmation screen
    · Score not shown to candidate immediately (shown after interviewer reviews)

--- INTERVIEWER VIEW — APTITUDE ROUND ---
  · After candidate submits, the interviewer sees the full results
  · Auto-graded MCQ score (correct / total)
  · Each question shown with candidate's answer and correct answer highlighted
  · Manual review section for any short-answer questions
  · Grading interface for manual answers (score input + comment)
  · Overall score and pass/fail threshold indicator
  · This view feeds directly into the feedback submission flow

--- ENHANCEMENT SUGGESTIONS ---
  · Add a session reconnect handler — if the candidate loses connection during
    a technical round, their code is auto-restored from the last 5-second
    autosave when they rejoin. Show a "Session restored" toast message.
  · For technical rounds, add a "Problem Statement" panel that the interviewer
    can toggle — it shows a pre-set coding problem description that both parties
    can see. This standardizes technical interviews and removes the need for
    screen sharing external documents.
  · For aptitude rounds, add a tab-switch warning — if the candidate switches
    to another browser tab, show a warning modal and log the event. Three
    tab-switch events trigger a notification to the interviewer.
  · Post-interview, auto-prompt the interviewer to submit feedback immediately
    after the workspace session ends — "Your interview ended. Submit feedback
    while it's fresh?" with a one-click shortcut to the feedback form.

MODULE 8 — NOTIFICATIONS SYSTEM
(Background Service — Visible via Email & Platform Toasts)
================================================================================

The Notifications System is the connective tissue of the platform. It ensures
every stakeholder is informed at every meaningful event. Notifications manifest
in two forms: email notifications (sent via SMTP) and in-platform toast/banner
notifications (real-time feedback on actions taken).

--- EMAIL NOTIFICATION TRIGGERS ---

  For Candidates:
    · Welcome email on signup confirmation
    · Onboarding wizard reminder (if not completed within 48 hours)
    · Application confirmation: "You have successfully applied to [Job Title]
      at [Company Name]"
    · Application status update: "Your application for [Job Title] has moved
      to [New Stage]"
    · Interview scheduled: "An interview has been scheduled for you — [Date],
      [Time], [Round Type]"
    · Interview rescheduled: New date/time notification
    · Interview cancelled: Cancellation with reason (if provided)
    · Application outcome: "Congratulations" (Offer/Hire) or "Thank you for
      your time" (Rejection) based on final pipeline stage

  For Internal Users (Recruiter / Interviewer):
    · Welcome/invite email with login credentials and login link
    · Interview assigned: "You have been assigned an interview — [Candidate],
      [Job], [Date], [Time]"
    · Interview rescheduled: Updated schedule notification
    · Interview cancelled: Cancellation notice
    · Feedback reminder: Sent 2 hours after interview completion if feedback
      is not yet submitted
    · New application alert: (optional, configurable) "A new candidate applied
      to [Job Title]"

  For Company Admin:
    · Company approved by Super Admin: "Your company workspace is ready"
    · Company rejected: Rejection reason email
    · New user invite confirmation: "Invite sent to [email]"

  For Super Admin:
    · New company registration pending approval

--- EMAIL TEMPLATE REQUIREMENTS ---
  · All emails use branded, clean HTML templates
  · Templates include: Platform logo, company name, action summary, CTA button,
    footer with unsubscribe/contact link
  · Dynamic content injected at send time (candidate name, job title, dates,
    status, etc.)
  · No plain-text emails — all HTML formatted

--- IN-APP TOAST NOTIFICATIONS ---
  · Success toasts (green): "Application submitted successfully"
  · Error toasts (red): "Failed to upload resume — please try again"
  · Info toasts (blue): "Interview scheduled successfully"
  · Warning toasts (yellow): "Your session is about to expire"
  · Position: Top-right corner, auto-dismiss after 5 seconds
  · All toasts are also dismissable via a close button

--- ENHANCEMENT SUGGESTIONS ---
  · Add an in-app notification bell icon in the top navbar for both candidate
    and company views. Clicking it opens a notification drawer showing the
    last 15 notification events with timestamps. Unread count shown as a
    badge. This dramatically improves engagement and reduces email fatigue.
  · Implement email preference settings — allow candidates and internal users
    to opt out of non-critical email types (e.g., opt out of application
    confirmation emails but keep interview schedule emails mandatory).
  · Add WhatsApp / SMS notification integration as a future enhancement for
    interview reminders — especially impactful for mobile-first candidates.

CROSS-CUTTING CONCERNS & GLOBAL UI PATTERNS
================================================================================

These are platform-wide UI principles that apply across all modules and all
user types. They establish consistency and ensure the application feels cohesive
regardless of which page the user is on.

--- GLOBAL NAVIGATION PATTERNS ---
  · Company/Internal View: Left sidebar (collapsible on smaller screens)
  · Candidate View: Top horizontal navbar (minimal, 4-5 items)
  · Super Admin View: Left sidebar (minimal, 4 items)
  · Breadcrumb navigation on all detail pages (e.g., Jobs → Frontend Engineer →
    Edit)

--- ERROR & EMPTY STATES ---
  Every list page and data visualization must have a thoughtful empty state:
  · No jobs posted yet: Illustration + "Create your first job posting" CTA
  · No candidates yet: Illustration + "Share your job to attract candidates"
  · No interviews scheduled: "Schedule your first interview" prompt
  · Empty analytics: "No data available yet. Activity will appear here as
    you post jobs and receive applications"
  All empty states include an appropriate icon/illustration, a headline,
  a short explanation sentence, and a CTA where applicable.

--- LOADING STATES ---
  · All data tables show skeleton loaders (grey animated rows) while fetching
  · All charts show a spinner or shimmer placeholder while loading
  · Buttons show a spinner icon + disabled state during async actions
  · No page should ever show a blank white screen during loading

--- FORM VALIDATION PATTERNS ---
  · All required fields show a red asterisk (*)
  · Validation runs on blur (when field loses focus), not on every keystroke
  · Error messages appear below the relevant field in red text
  · On form submit, all errors are shown simultaneously and the page scrolls
    to the first errored field
  · Success actions show a green toast notification

--- RESPONSIVE DESIGN ---
  · All pages are designed mobile-first
  · Tables on mobile collapse to a card-based stacked view
  · Sidebars on mobile collapse to a slide-out drawer triggered by a hamburger
    menu icon
  · Charts on mobile show a simplified version (e.g., donut replaces funnel,
    horizontal bar replaces grouped bar)
  · Wizard forms stack vertically on mobile, full-width inputs

--- ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (UI LEVEL) ---
  · Routes are protected at both the API and UI level
  · If a user tries to access a URL they don't have permission for, they see
    a clean 403 "Access Denied" page with a "Go Back" button
  · Navigation items not accessible to a role are not rendered in the sidebar
    (not just hidden — removed from DOM)
  · Role is decoded from the JWT token and stored in client-side state
  · Token expiry triggers an automatic logout with a "Session expired" message

DATA MODEL SUMMARY (FOR REFERENCE)
================================================================================

The following tables form the database foundation of the entire application.
All UI data flows originate from and write back to these structures.

  companies:     id, name, domain, company_size, industry, address fields,
                 contact_email, contact_phone, status (pending/active/rejected),
                 active (boolean), admin_user_id (FK → users)

  users:         id, email, password_digest, name, company_id (FK → companies)

  roles:         id, name (Super Admin / Admin / Recruiter / Interviewer)
  memberships:   user_id + role_id (many-to-many bridge)

  jobs:          id, title, description, status (draft/published/closed),
                 department, location, experience_level, required_skills[],
                 company_id, created_by_id

  candidates:    id, name, email, phone, password_digest, location, resume_url,
                 linkedin_url, github_url, portfolio_url, status, resume_text,
                 skills[], ai_match_score, summary, resume_data (jsonb),
                 experience (jsonb), preferences (jsonb), current_step (1-4),
                 onboarding_completed (boolean), profile_completion (0-100),
                 profile_strength_score

  applications:  id, job_id, candidate_id, user_id (recruiter), status,
                 applied_at, resume_url (snapshot), cover_note, ai_score,
                 parsed_skills[]

  interviews:    id, application_id, round_type, interviewer_id, scheduled_at,
                 status (scheduled/completed/cancelled), meeting_link

  feedbacks:     id, interview_id, rating (1-5), strengths, weaknesses,
                 recommendation (strong_hire/hire/no_hire/strong_no_hire)

Pipeline Status Progression:
  applied → screening → shortlisted → interview_1 → interview_2 →
  offer → hired | rejected

================================================================================
FINAL NOTES FOR UI AGENTS BUILDING FROM THIS STORY
================================================================================

This document is the single source of truth for all UI design and development
on this platform. When building any page:

  1. Every page has a defined purpose. Do not add decorative sections or
     modules that are not described here. Minimalism is intentional.

  2. Every form field has a reason. The data model is tight and normalized.
     Do not add fields that do not map to the schema above.

  3. Role determines experience. Before rendering any page, resolve the user's
     role and render accordingly. The same URL may behave differently for
     a Recruiter vs. a Company Admin.

  4. Empty states matter. Every list, table, and chart needs an empty state.
     Blank screens are not acceptable.

  5. The Wizard flows (Candidate Onboarding + Company Onboarding) are
     resumable. Store step progress server-side. Do not rely on localStorage
     alone — a user switching devices should pick up where they left off.

  6. The Live Interview Workspace is the premium differentiator. It should
     feel polished, fast, and focused. Code editor, video call, and assessment
     panel are all first-class experiences within this workspace.

  7. Notifications are invisible infrastructure. They must fire reliably for
     every listed trigger event. Email delivery failures must be logged and
     retried. The user should never feel "abandoned" after an action.

  8. Analytics tell a story. Charts are not decorative. Every chart should
     answer a real question a recruiter or admin would have about their
     hiring performance.

  9. This is a hackathon-grade, portfolio-quality application built to
     production standards. Every screen should be something you would be
     proud to show in a live demo.
